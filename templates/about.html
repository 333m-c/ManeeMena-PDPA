Personal data deserves care

Thailand’s Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) addresses the collection, use and disclosure of personal data. This project explores one practical step: reducing the information exposed in text logs.

Veil applies five readable regex rules. It is an educational demonstration of partial masking, not a tool that certifies legal compliance or guarantees anonymity.

Temporary by design

Your data is processed temporarily and is not stored.

  • Text travels to this application’s Python server for processing.
  • No database, saved scan history or browser storage.
  • No raw input in application logs or analytics.
  • No third-party processing, fonts, scripts or tracking.
  • Copy and download happen only when you choose.

Use Clear to discard the current workspace. If you host the app elsewhere, that server receives the text; configure its transport and infrastructure accordingly.

{{ icon('code') }}

Know the boundaries

Only the enabled patterns are counted. A “Safe” demo score means zero matches, not proof that a log contains no personal data.

  • Card and phone numbers require the specified hyphens.
  • DOB and Address require their exact, case-sensitive labels.
  • Dates are checked for shape, not calendar validity.
  • Short email usernames (one or two characters) stay visible.
  • Names and the rest of an address remain unchanged.

Review the output before using it. Regex cannot understand every way personal data can appear.
